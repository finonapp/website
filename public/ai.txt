Brand Messaging:
Finon focuses on helping people see what their finances add up to without switching between banking, investment and crypto apps.
It provides a read-only view: funds stay with the user's bank, broker or exchange. Finon cannot move money or place trades and does not provide financial or investment advice.

Key Value Proposition:
- Bank balances, investments and crypto in one dashboard
- Portfolio history and asset allocation
- Connections to supported banks, investment platforms and crypto exchanges
- Statement imports from Freetrade and Moneybox, updated by uploading new statements
- Manual entry for commodities such as gold and silver
- Free plan with up to 2 connections, with Finon Pro available for additional connections
